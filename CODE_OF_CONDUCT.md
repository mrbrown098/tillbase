# Code of Conduct

Tillbase is built by and for people who run shops and restaurants, and by volunteers from many countries and backgrounds. We want everyone to be able to take part without being belittled, harassed or ignored.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)), in shorter words.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to code, to English, or to the project.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly when something is unclear. Many of us write in a second language.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific, and offer a way forward.
- Accept feedback gracefully. A pull request that is declined is not a verdict on you.
- Respect people's time, privacy and shops: do not share a shop owner's data or screenshots with real customer or staff details.
- Be honest about what you tested and what you did not.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or demeaning jokes, including about someone's language, accent, country, religion, gender, sexuality, age, disability, appearance, race or background.
- Sexualised language or images, unwelcome attention, or unwelcome contact.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging violence.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or phone number, without their permission.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or pushing a product in issues and pull requests. (Sponsorship has its own, public process, described in `ads/README.md`.)
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, comments and reviews), and in any space where someone is clearly representing Tillbase.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you are being treated badly or you see it happen, report it **privately** to the maintainers using GitHub's private reporting: the **Security** tab of this repository, then **Report a vulnerability**, and start the message with "Code of Conduct". (That is the one private channel this project has today; it will be replaced with a dedicated address when there is one.) Reports are read by the maintainers, handled confidentially, and answered, with the reporter's name kept private.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban a person. They explain the decision to the people involved. Maintainers who break this code, or who have a conflict of interest in a report, are handled by the other maintainers.
